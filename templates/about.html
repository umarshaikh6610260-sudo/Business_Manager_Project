ABOUT BUSINESSMANAGER

Simple software for real business work.

BusinessManager brings customers, products, quotations, invoices, payments, expenses and reports together in one practical business system.

WHY WE BUILT IT

Business information should not be scattered everywhere.

Many small and growing businesses manage their daily work using notebooks, spreadsheets, messages and separate applications.

BusinessManager is designed to bring those everyday tasks into one organized place. The goal is simple: spend less time searching for information and more time actually running the business.

From a customer's purchase history to an unpaid invoice, important information stays connected and easy to find.
